## 2. Choosing who you're for

**Market comes first**
- Order of importance: market, then offer, then persuasion. Check you are not in a bad market, then move on. [3 Ways To Increase Wealth Through Business Now]
- The VC line he repeats: great operator plus bad market, the market wins. [GROWTH LEVERS: Big Business Owner Explains Real World Tactics That Work]
- Four things every market needs: massive pain, purchasing power, easy to target, growing. Want all four, not three. [The Key To Finding a Profitable Market]
- Three of four still fails: a resume service had growth, pain and easy targeting, but unemployed buyers had no money. It tanked. [GROWTH LEVERS: Big Business Owner Explains Real World Tactics That Work]
- Growth does the work for you: a newspaper rev-share seller with a good offer lost to a shrinking market, then the same person boomed selling masks. [The Key To Finding a Profitable Market]
- Value depends on who you ask. A problem is worth more to someone rich. Same service, different buyer, different price. [I increased my wealth EXPONENTIALLY using the power of INVERSE thinking...]
- Selling a valuable service partly to broke people and partly to rich people? Sell to the rich. [$100M Money Models Live Launch]

**Get narrow, then widen**
- Specificity sets price: time management is a cheap PDF; for salespeople, more; for outbound reps in garden and power tools, a $10,000 system with almost no competitors. His numbers are illustrative. ["I'm Broke, What Business Do I Start?"]
- Niche versus big market, reconciled: start as the "biggest guy in a puddle" where sharks aren't, then step up to pond, lake, ocean. [You can make way more money than you think]
- Crowded isn't a reason to stay out. Bloody water means more fish, and often the biggest rewards. [You can make way more money than you think]
- A niche can really saturate. Gym Launch had talked to a big share of its micro gyms, and bigger founders he met were simply in bigger markets. [The REAL Reason Your Business Isn't Growing]

**Find the avatar in your own data**
- List past customers in four columns: love them, spent most, most operating profit, easiest to deliver. Whoever lands in two or three is the avatar. [Business Was Hard Until I Understood These 4 Concepts]
- Describe three things: who they are, what they were doing on arrival, what they did after that made them succeed. Ask which one type you'd clone. [Business is hard until you do this]
- When adjacent avatars tie, pick the one you make the most dollars for. [The REAL Reason Your Business Isn't Growing]
- Your best customer may surprise you: Allan was built for small businesses, and agency owners turned out best. [How to Build a Business That Runs Without You]
- "They don't pay enough" is not a reason to drop a type you'd like to serve. You set the price. [Business is hard until you do this]

**Commit to it**
- Then realign everything: offer, headlines, testimonials, onboarding. Sales may not sell anyone else. [Business is hard until you do this]
- Gym Launch's filter was objective facts: gym owner, signed lease, at least one employee, at least 30 members. It went in ads, pages and first lines on calls. CAC barely moved, LTV jumped. [If You Missed it... I'm Launching Live. Again]
- Say who it isn't for. He rates that above calling out who it is for, because it shows where you sit in the market. [14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes]
- Qualifying acts like a niche of its own: the "maybe" hears you only serve people like them, and leans in. [I Blew Up A Secret Business To Prove It’s Not Luck]

**If you're just starting**
- Common error is picking a niche you know nothing about. He cites Y Combinator weighing past experience in the space. [The 10 Minute MILLIONAIRE entrepreneur advice for people starting from ZERO]
- His rule: something you aren't repelled by, that you see others making money in, then stay an unusually long time. [The 10 Minute MILLIONAIRE entrepreneur advice for people starting from ZERO]
- You don't need a new category. Only someone inventing one can blame "people don't want it"; in an existing industry it's a skill gap. [Get Rich While You’re Young | Alex Hormozi Answers Your Questions Live]

## 3. Standing apart from competitors

**Be one of one, in a pond you pick**
- Goal: "category of one", so a buyer has nothing to compare and makes a value decision, not a price decision. [GROWTH LEVERS: Big Business Owner Explains Real World Tactics That Work]
- Narrowing helps because it shrinks the pool you compete against. [I increased my wealth EXPONENTIALLY using the power of INVERSE thinking...]
- You control the pond. Only triple black belt locally? Only one who's helped a very specific kind of client? Say so. [14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes]
- Competitors can copy an offer, not proof. Real experience is the defense, more so as AI content spreads. [14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes]
- If only you can see the differentiator, it isn't one. A costly supplement formula lost to mediocre ones because buyers couldn't tell. [Sick of Shiny Object Syndrome? Watch This]

**Better is often enough**
- Marketing is about different, the business about better. You don't need a blue ocean product; it just has to be better. [Explaining How Anyone Can Make $100M® Offers]
- He says he does nothing new, only does old things better. You can win in dry cleaning or lawn care that way. [The 10 Minute MILLIONAIRE entrepreneur advice for people starting from ZERO]
- Frozen yogurt chains are nearly the same, poorly run and dirty, so a commodity industry today can still be out-competed. [title unavailable, video xZ8d9g6BcKM]

**Four positions in any market**
- Bargain (cheapest, poor experience), best value (Target over Walmart), premium (Lexus), luxury (no price cap; part of the value is others knowing what you paid). [How The World’s RICHEST Man Made His Money]
- Cheapest is a legitimate strategy only if you commit to it. Low-cost leaders build the business around efficiency. [Give Me 42 Minutes, I'll Show You How To Sell Like The Top 1% | Scale or Fail Ep]
- Agency example: a strategically low $300-500 a month run on VAs and AI, or move up to real businesses at a real price. [If You Missed it... I'm Launching Live. Again]

**If you're smaller**
- Every position has an advantage. Unknown means nimble, unlimited shots, and small rivals. [Your biggest advantage is no one knows who you are]
- Us versus them suits the challenger. Number one shouldn't do it; a leader runs us versus the alternative. Same for side-by-side price comparisons. [STEAL Ryan Reynolds' $1.3B Marketing Strategy [NO FAME REQUIRED]]
- You can't beat a giant nationally. Beat it in one market on service, as Avis did with "we try harder". [Alex Hormozi Answers Your Questions]
- Take the big brand's story apart: at a prestige firm you get a sliver of a partner and junior analysts; with me, my cell phone. [How To Think Like The Top 1% | Sales Masterclass]
- Script form in references/scripts-and-swipes.md, "Underdog Positioning Script".

**How to treat competitors**
- Don't react. He once changed his model in response to a rival and only hurt himself; most customers never knew. [How I CRUSH copycats, thieves and competition.]
- Never name them. "Coke never acknowledges Pepsi." If they get loud, get louder about your own thing. [How I CRUSH copycats, thieves and competition.]
- Most dangerous competitor test: what would the rival you'd least want to face do in your spot? Do that. (references/decisions-and-patterns.md)
- Structural moats (network effects, data, licensing, scale, brand) are a separate list. [13 Ways to DESTROY Your Competition (Legally)]

**Thin here:** nothing on competitor research as a method, category design, or messaging frameworks. Most advice comes from live Q&A about one caller's business.

## 4. Not being a commodity

**The test**
- Can a buyer hold your thing next to theirs, call them the same, and take the cheaper? Then you sell a commodity. Services count. [How To Learn Anything Faster Than Everyone Else]
- Averaging competitors' prices guarantees it. So does a little more for a little less. [Alex Hormozi Answers Your Questions on Reddit]
- Using the same measured unit as everyone, like an hourly rate, makes you one. [Give Me 42 Minutes, I'll Show You How To Sell Like The Top 1% | Scale or Fail Ep]
- The end state: everyone gives more for less until they're effectively nonprofits. [This video will make you more money than anything else on the Internet]
- Thin margins may be a symptom of a commodity offer, not a cost problem. [How To Learn Anything Faster Than Everyone Else]

**The way out**
- Add a service layer to a product so you're judged alone. [How To Make BUCKETS of Money In SERVICE Businesses]
- Build value around the core: speed, rework, risk. Ask "if we all cost the same, which would you pick?" Then that's why you cost more. [Answering Your Sales Question Live]
- Local versus global: dearer today, cheaper over the haul. Screws can be sold this way. It must be true. [Get Rich While You’re Young | Alex Hormozi Answers Your Questions Live]
- Combinations make things rare; breaking them into chunks commoditizes them. [58 Mins of Advice That Will Blow Up Your Business]
- Brand: the gap above the commodity price measures its power. Same white T-shirt, logo or not. [Why MrBeast Will be Worth $100 Billion]
- Pair a commodity with meaning: jewelry or a shirt plus the story behind it. [We get 34.2M Views a Month. Here's how (just copy me)]
- Sow doubt in the comparison when stuck: flat cap against their hourly, or tiered, tracked staff. [Give Me 42 Minutes, I'll Show You How To Sell Like The Top 1% | Scale or Fail Ep]
- Case told by him: agencies with a commoditized retainer moved to a decommoditized offer and closed about 2.3x as many appointments at a higher price. [GROWTH LEVERS: Big Business Owner Explains Real World Tactics That Work]

**Where it's fine, or coming anyway**
- A bookkeeper can stay commodity: on time and responsive is enough to get kept. [Advice for Hard Days | 1/0 LIVE Episode 1]
- Hot products commoditize: he compares semaglutide to Botox getting chipped down. [Brutally Honest Advice For Mental Toughness | Hormozi Hotline]
- Asset creation is now commoditized by AI, so judgment and strategy carry the value. [Alex Hormozi Answers Your Questions]
- CAC tends to be a commodity within an industry. LTV is what separates businesses. [How To Learn Anything Faster Than Everyone Else]

## 5. Naming and framing an offer

**Naming**
- MAGIC: magnetic reason why, avatar, goal, time interval, container word. Full version and examples in references/offers/naming-niching.md. [How to get rich this Black Friday]
- Name for the buyer's ego, not your taste. "Gym Rescue" triggered pushback on calls; "Gym Launch" for the same service didn't. [If I Wanted to Get Rich and Famous, This Is What I'd Do]
- He calls naming and packaging possibly the most important step, citing a dating book that tanked, then sold millions with a new cover and headline. Secondhand story. [Giving Away Free Stuff Will Make You Rich]
- Tracking what you already do can become a story: level-three movers with a set number of jobs. [Give Me 42 Minutes, I'll Show You How To Sell Like The Top 1% | Scale or Fail Ep]

**Same offer, new wrapper**
- Once an offer wins, keep it and change the wrapper with the season. [STEAL Ryan Reynolds' $1.3B Marketing Strategy [NO FAME REQUIRED]]
- Rotate names so non-buyers see something new: new page, subject line, challenge name. [This Email Campaign Generates Sales [Full Breakdown]]
- Six-week, 42-day, 21-day: the wrapper changes, nutrition and workouts stay. [I Helped 6 Business Owners Make More Money]

**Framing**
- Sell the outcome, not the membership: a promised result by a date, not $99 a month. [10x Revenue with 1 New Sales Process (You Can Steal It)]
- Price against the dream outcome, not an hourly rate nobody can judge. [I Blew Up a Business Through 3 Small Changes (Just Copy Me)]
- Listing deliverables can cheapen it. "A 22 email setup" sounds commoditized. [$100M Money Models Live Launch]
- Count the beliefs a buyer must hold to buy, then reposition or repackage until it's one or none. [How To Get Customers So Fast It Feels ILLEGAL]
- Lead with what they respond to, even if you'd rather sell the sustainable truth. [Marketing Mastery - How To Get Your Ideal Clients To Come To You]
- Equipment gets no pitch of its own. A $20,000 suit changes nothing except a benefit: less time. [#1 Mistake I'm Seeing Gyms Make Right Now [ALEX HORMOZI]]
- Borrowed status shifts position too: "pseudo medical" positioning gives more price flexibility. ["He Makes $1.5M a Year... and Keeps 1.3% of It" | Alex Hormozi Answers Live]
- Others introducing you beats saying it yourself. [14 Life Lessons I Wish I Knew Earlier]
- A premium promise dies if the sales process runs like a commodity's. [I Blew Up A Secret Business To Prove It’s Not Luck]

## 6. How price signals position

- Aim so high the buyer thinks something different must be going on. That pulls you out of the comparison. [GROWTH LEVERS: Big Business Owner Explains Real World Tactics That Work]
- Price alone can place you in another category and make people look closer. [6 Hacks To Make More Money [Increase LTV] Without More Customers]
- Two cases where raising price can raise closes. One: Veblen goods, where the price is the appeal. Two: normal businesses, where higher price raises belief you can deliver. [Building a $2,500,000 Business for a Stranger in 36 Minutes]
- Premium health and cosmetic offers can behave like Veblen goods, and going up market funds better talent. [This Video Should Be Required Viewing for Business Owners]
- Better prospects make the product better: same service, abler people, more results. Price raises the bar. [How To Upsell More Customers By Reversing Your Pricing]
- Price is also relative to the buyer: $2,000 can be too cheap to matter to one person and everything to another. [Alex Hormozi Answers Your Questions on Reddit]
- Cheap competitor: price is what you pay, value is what you get. Then ask which they'd pick at equal price, and let them argue your case. [Say THIS When Your Competition is CHEAPER]
- A cheaper rival often isn't a real threat. Low LTV means they can't afford to acquire customers. [Say THIS When Your Competition is CHEAPER]
- Moving tiers moves price: up market about 5-10x, down market about a fifth to a tenth. [Scaling Business Explained With One Formula]
- Artisan path: commodity, premium, then luxury price as skill grows. [3 Ways to Do What You Love (and get wealthy too)]
- Anchor: a costly defined program makes later continuity feel cheap. [How To Upsell More Customers By Reversing Your Pricing]

## 7. When to reposition

**First rule out the wrong diagnosis**
- Most common error: reading a bottleneck on one channel as the limit of the market. Add a channel or raise gross profit per customer first. [Scaling Business Explained With One Formula]
- "Saturated" is the top excuse he hears, and he says 99% who say it are wrong. [You can make way more money than you think]

**Signals to move**
- The market is shrinking: that's the kind of hard you can't fix, so pivot. [The Top 1% of Business Owners Aren't Smarter Than You]
- Your real buyers are too few or broke: at Allan, gyms with enough leads were tiny and poor, and agencies were the better buyer. [This Video Will Make Your Business Unstoppable]
- Around $1M to $3M, a mix of promises and prices becomes the sticking point. Time for the customer audit. [Business Was Hard Until I Understood These 4 Concepts]
- You keep hearing the same flinch on calls: change the words, as with Gym Rescue. [If I Wanted to Get Rich and Famous, This Is What I'd Do]
- Your tier is commoditizing: create the next thing. A guest describes doing this with a hybrid offer. [I've met Arnold 4 times. Here's what I learned.]
- A valuation or scale cap: a small TAM is an invisible ceiling. Reach for this at $1M to $10M, not before. [Scaling Business Explained With One Formula]

**How to move**
- Up, adjacent, down, or new-opportunity offer. Each changes delivery and prospect quality. [Scaling Business Explained With One Formula]
- Going up needs a real price jump: fewer buyers expecting more. Going down needs less service; he cut price, kept delivery and it failed. [Scaling Business Explained With One Formula]
- Salon example of five directions, with narrower as the one that shrinks the market yet can raise revenue. [The REAL Reason Your Business Isn't Growing]
- Reposition the existing stuff before building: repackage, rename, change terms. He says half a day to a day. [3 Ways To Increase Wealth Through Business Now]
- Move a piece's job: cheap education became free education feeding the services. [I Blew Up a Business Through 3 Small Changes (Just Copy Me)]
- Small rewording: an unstated call becomes a named assessment. [Building a $1,000,000 Business for a Stranger in 56 Mins]
- Be nimble early. Changing an offer with few customers is far easier than with many. [Your biggest advantage is no one knows who you are]
- Expect short-term pain: he says you sometimes walk down the mountain to walk back up. [Business is hard until you do this]

**Thin here:** the signals are scattered anecdotes, not a checklist. He gives no rule for moving existing customers through a new position. Results quoted in launches are sales pitch, not evidence.

## 8. How to test it

**Thin. No video gives a positioning test.** What follows is nearby material; applying it to positioning is my inference, not his.

- Prices: his portfolio method is simply to test on calls. Go low to high; down is harder. [Make More Profit than 99% of People]
- Raise it, and if sales fall, lower it. Not testing is the costly mistake. [Building a $3,500,000 Business for a Stranger in 51 Mins]
- Testing often correlates with profit, per a chart he credits to ProfitWell. Secondhand. [How To Think Like The Top 1% | Sales Masterclass]
- Payment plans as a gauge: nudge the anchor up until people freak out. [23 - Payment Plans - 100M Money Models Course]
- Names and headlines: he split tested book names, images and subheads before writing it. Two days of testing for two years of writing. [Watch This To Generate 1000s of Leads (In Any Niche)]
- Demand: pre-selling is the cleaner check. If nobody wants it, you've learned that. [I Live Streamed For 30 Days. Here's What I Learned.]
- Avatar: back-test on past customers using the four columns before cutting anyone. [Business Was Hard Until I Understood These 4 Concepts]
- Filter: Gym Launch's result suggests what to watch, CAC against LTV. [If You Missed it... I'm Launching Live. Again]
- Differentiator: ask whether customers can recognize it unprompted. Inferred from the supplement story. [Sick of Shiny Object Syndrome? Watch This]
- Not covered at all: niche test design, sample sizes, message testing with interviews, or measuring whether a position has landed.
